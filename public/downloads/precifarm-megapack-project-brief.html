Precifarm MegaPack is a project-engineered battery energy storage system (BESS) platform for commercial, industrial, renewable-energy, EV-charging, microgrid and utility-scale applications in Kenya — with a design path across Africa.

Precifarm is the designer, integrator and project developer — and may be a financing or operating partner where that is lawful and contracted. We do not claim to manufacture cells, power conversion equipment or complete containers as a standard catalogue product.

Applications

Industrial & commercial

Peak shaving, backup, process continuity and solar firming at factories, cold chain and campuses.

Utility & grid-connected

Project-scoped plants subject to interconnection studies — not advertised as net-metering or guaranteed grid revenue.

EV hub & corridor energy

Buffer storage for depot and highway charging where grid import limits or solar surplus need orchestration.

Microgrid & weak-grid sites

Islandable or grid-following architectures where Kenya Power availability and critical loads dictate design.

What to tell us

  • What you need to power, and which loads are critical
  • How much energy to store, and for how many hours
  • Connection point (voltage, capacity, export if any)
  • Solar or other generation on site or planned
  • Whether EV charging, backup or grid-support is in scope
  • Preferred commercial model (own, finance, service)

How we work

Assess → Model → Connect → Engineer → Finance → Build → Operate. Final architecture depends on grid voltage, connection capacity, load profile, export capability, battery power and energy, protection, metering, utility requirements, operating model and applicable Kenyan regulations.

Regulatory position

Designed for compliance with applicable Kenyan requirements. Final compliance and approvals are determined project-by-project. Grid-connected projects require site-specific engineering, utility interconnection, applicable EPRA approvals/licensing, environmental and county approvals, and the relevant network operator.

Precifarm does not claim “EPRA certified”, “KPLC approved”, net-metering for utility BESS, or licensed energy-storage operator status on this brief. Backup is not automatic on a grid-tied BESS. Storage can reduce instantaneous grid demand; it does not eliminate interconnection studies or possible reinforcement.
